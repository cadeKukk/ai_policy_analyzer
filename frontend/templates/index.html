National AI Initiative Act Overview

The National Artificial Intelligence Initiative Act (NAIIA) of 2020 provides a coordinated program across the federal government to accelerate AI research and application for economic prosperity and national security.

Key Components:
  • Establishes the National AI Initiative Office within the White House
  • Creates an advisory committee with representatives from industry, academia, and civil society
  • Directs federal agencies to increase investment in AI research and development
  • Supports development of educational resources for various stakeholders
  • Establishes research institutes focused on AI applications in different domains
